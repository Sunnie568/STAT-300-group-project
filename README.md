Student academic performance is traditionally evaluated through final exam scores, but it also heavily influenced by various demographic, social, and school-related factors. Understanding these relationships can help educators identify key characteristics associated with academic success and target students who may need additional support.
A central question in educational research is understanding what factors contribute to student academic success. While core academic habits like study time and previous grades are critical, extracurricular engagement is often believed to play a vital role in holistic student development.
This study aims to investigate whether participation in extracurricular activities has a statistically significant effect on students' final academic performance, measured by their final grade.
